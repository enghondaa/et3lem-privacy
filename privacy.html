Privacy Policy – Et3lem Comments Assistant

Last updated: November 20, 2026

The Et3lem Comments Assistant is a private tool used only by the owner of the Et3lem (اتعلم) YouTube channel and Facebook page to read public comments on Et3lem's own videos and posts and to publish short replies on behalf of the channel.

Data we access

  • Public comments (text, comment ID, commenter display name) left on Et3lem's own YouTube videos and Facebook page posts.
  • Basic channel/page identifiers needed to post replies.

How we use it

Comment text is sent to an AI model (Anthropic Claude) only to draft a short reply. We do not sell, share or use the data for advertising, and we do not build profiles of users.

Storage

We store only the IDs of comments that were already answered, to avoid replying twice. No personal data is kept beyond that.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
